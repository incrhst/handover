How this works
  • Everything stays on this Mac, in your data folder. Nothing is sent anywhere. The app only asks GitHub whether a new version is out.
  • This is a transition kit: one file that works in any browser, with no app and no internet. It never sends anything anywhere. It is a copy of the handover and doesn't change when the handover does.
  • Tick tasks as you go. Recurring duties come back on their schedule; anything missed shows as overdue.
  • Each morning a task sheet for the day is saved in your data folder.
  • Reminders come as notifications: one each morning with what's to do, and one when a job with a time is due. Click Done on a reminder to tick the job off.
  • Yellow boxes are information the author still has to fill in.
  • Press / to search, or ⌘F in the app.
Settings
Data folder:

Choose a folder in iCloud Drive, Google Drive or Dropbox to back it up automatically. A dated copy is also kept every night.

To work on a handover together, share that folder with the other person and have them choose it here too. Changes made on either Mac show up on the other within seconds of the cloud service copying them across, and changes made at the same time are combined. Each person keeps their own ticks and notes.

Notifications are turned off for Handover, so no reminders will show.

Each regular job shows in a Reminders list named after the handover, a week before it's due (or when its work has to start). Tick it there, on your iPhone or here: a tick in one place shows in the other. Only the handover that is open is kept up to date.

Handover isn't allowed to use Reminders, so the list isn't kept up to date.

Your ticks and notes are kept in this browser only. To carry on in the Handover app, use Share & export, Export with my progress, and open that file in the app.
Running in a browser: progress is saved in this browser only. Install the Mac app for files on disk, backups and the daily sheet.
