How this works
  • Everything stays on this Mac, in your data folder. Nothing is sent anywhere. The app only asks GitHub whether a new version is out.
  • Tick tasks as you go. Recurring duties come back on their schedule; anything missed shows as overdue.
  • Each morning a task sheet for the day is saved in your data folder.
  • Yellow boxes are information the author still has to fill in.
  • Press / to search, or ⌘F in the app.
Settings
